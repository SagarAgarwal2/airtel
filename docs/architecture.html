Udyam Digital: solution architecture

One Journey API keeps the advisor, quote, KYC and install status under a single case ID. Only the rules engine sets prices, and KYC documents never reach the language model.

Journey API (FastAPI)

GenAI advisorAsks 6 questions (type, city/PIN and sites, staff, tools, GSTIN, budget). The LLM extracts fields and phrases the explanation; a rule-based parser works without a key.
Rules & pricing engineCity tier, minimum staff and sites, active GSTIN, product dependencies, catalogue prices, budget fit. Explains every item it leaves out.
Quote & checkoutOne monthly price, add-on toggles, server-side re-pricing, idempotency keys on order and booking
Smart KYCConsent first. Blur and skew checks, OCR, then GSTIN checksum, registry status, name, address, Udyam, bill age and signatory. Result is auto-clear, fix or human review.
Order trackerInstall slot, mock UPI autopay, milestones posted by ops, per-product status and ETA
Propensity scoreLogistic model on lead fields. AUC 0.70; the top 20% of leads hold 44% of activations.
Privacy layerConsent ledger (versioned) · IDs masked in UI and logs · documents encrypted and auto-deleted after 24 h · audit trail · delete-my-documents
